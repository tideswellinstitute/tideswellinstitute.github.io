By Car

Tideswell is easily accessible via the A6 or A623. Free parking is available along the High Street and in the village square, though we recommend arriving early during peak summer months.

By Train & Bus

The nearest rail stations are Buxton and Hope. From there, the 65 and 173 bus services run regular routes through the village, stopping just a two-minute walk from our door.

Accessibility

Due to the historic nature of our Grade II listed building, access is via a small step at the main entrance. We have portable ramps available—please call ahead so we can assist your arrival.

Note:

The Institute is located directly opposite the famous 'Cathedral of the Peak' (St John the Baptist Church). If you reach the church gates, you have found us.
