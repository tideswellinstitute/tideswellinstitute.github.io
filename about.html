A Legacy of Learning

The Tideswell Institute was built to provide the village with a space for mental improvement and social connection. Today, we carry that mission forward into the digital age.

Tideswell High Street, circa 1900

Built for the People

Originally founded as a Reading Room and Library, the Institute served as the intellectual heart of Tideswell for generations. It was a place where local residents could access the day's news, share ideas, and escape the rigours of industrial life.

Our Grade II listed building retains its original character—the vaulted ceilings, heavy oak doors, and the very same windows that looked out over the village square over 150 years ago.

Preservation through Purpose

We believe the best way to save historic buildings is to make them useful. By transforming the Institute into a modern co-working space, we ensure the building remains heated, maintained, and vibrant for another century.
