The Institute

Reception Hire

A historic venue in the centre of Tideswell, available for private hire and wedding receptions.

The Meeting Hall

The Meeting Hall is a Grade II listed space featuring vaulted ceilings and Victorian stone windows. It provides a formal yet simple setting for wedding receptions and community gatherings.

Kitchen & Facilities

Hire of the hall includes full access to the on-site kitchen. The space is suitable for external caterers or for the independent preparation of refreshments.

General Terms of Hire

The Institute operates on a "dry hire" basis. We provide the hall, kitchen, and grounds; all other arrangements—including catering, decor, and furniture—are the responsibility of the hirer.

As a charity-run building within a residential village, we ask that all users respect our local surroundings. We do not charge corkage, and hirers are welcome to arrange their own provision of drinks.

The Garden

To the rear of the building is a private walled garden. It offers a quiet outdoor space with views of the Parish Church, suitable for photographs and drinks.

Inquiries

Hire rates are set to support the ongoing maintenance of the building. Please contact the Wardens to discuss availability or to arrange a viewing of the facilities.
