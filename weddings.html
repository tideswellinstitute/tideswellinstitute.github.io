From the timeless stone architecture to the soft light of the Peak District, the Institute offers a soulful backdrop for smaller, meaningful celebrations.

The Reading Room

Our main hall, with its vaulted timber ceiling and arched windows, provides a sacred atmosphere for ceremonies of up to 50 guests. The acoustics of the Victorian stonework are perfect for acoustic musicians or intimate vows.

As the sun sets over the village, the room is bathed in a warm, golden hue, requiring very little decoration to feel complete.

The Secret Garden

Hidden behind the Institute lies our private walled garden. It is an ideal space for champagne receptions or outdoor photography, with views of the 'Cathedral of the Peak' rising just beyond the stone walls.
